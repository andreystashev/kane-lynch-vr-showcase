Kane & Lynch 2 VR x86 D3D9On12 selector.
Built reproducibly by build-on12-renderer.ps1 from src/renderers/.
Loads the Windows D3D9On12 implementation from the system directory.
No Microsoft runtime binaries are redistributed.
The process-local policy bounds temporary upload batches to 8 MiB and idle
buffer retention to 5 completed fences. Other native compatibility fields
are preserved. Unknown setter implementations keep normal Windows behavior.
Diagnostic probe files are disabled by default. Researchers may explicitly
set the process environment KL2_VR_RENDERER_LOG=1 before creating a factory.
